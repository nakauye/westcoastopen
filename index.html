About the tournament

Two Colors.
One Tatami.

Every match at the Westcoast Open is scored the traditional way — aka on the red side, ao in the blue. Aka and Ao face each other. Aka on the referees right side for every limit and kata match. That rule shapes how we run the whole event: clear divisions, fair brackets, and a competition floor open to first-year students and seasoned black belts alike.

Whether you're stepping onto a tournament mat for the first time or chasing a regional title, you'll compete in a division sized to your age and rank.

AKA

Red Side

Called first to the mat for every kumite and kata match.

AO

Blue Side

Second call, opposite side of the ring or performance line.

#

Repechage Bracket

Each bracket consist of two pools with third place determined by winner between the two pools.

A Letter From The Tournament Director

Welcome

Dear Sensei, Coach, and Athlete,

We are pleased and honored to invite you to participate in the 2026 West Coast Open Martial Arts Championships on Sunday, October 11, 2026. This year's event will once again be held at the beautiful Pierce College Gymnasium in Lakewood, WA — a perfect venue that allows us to organize an enjoyable event for everyone.

This competition will feature senseis and competitors from Canada and across the USA, including Hawaii, Alaska, Washington, Oregon, California, Colorado, Montana, and Idaho. The quality of the competition and officiating promises once again to be the finest in the USA.

We are proud to have the assistance and sponsorship of the Pierce County South Sound Sports Commission and appreciate their hard work in making this a successful event. We are also grateful to our ring and medal sponsors, whose support helps greatly to enhance the experience of parents, spectators, and athletes.

Our referee corps are amongst the best in the USA and are committed to ensuring a fair and safe playing field for everyone. Our volunteer staff, as you all know, provide the accommodating support that ensures a smooth-running tournament.

All competitors, officials, and volunteers are welcome to participate in this goodwill event, so mark your calendars for Sunday, October 11th, and join us at the 2026 West Coast Open International Martial Arts Championships.
